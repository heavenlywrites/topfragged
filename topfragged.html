Rank Map Cover Maker

A map-cover styled overlay that shows your live rank, RR, and region on top of any background you pick. It checks your rank on its own and keeps the overlay current while you stream, no manual updates.

How it works
  • 1Add your Riot ID, region, and a free API key, plus a background image if you want one.
  • 2It builds you one link with everything baked in.
  • 3Paste that link into OBS as a Browser Source. Your rank updates itself from there.

Valorant Spinner

Spin to get your next challenge: a random agent and a random number of games to play them for. It hands you a stream overlay showing exactly what you landed on.

How it works
  • 1Hit spin. It randomly locks in an agent and a game count, like "Sage for 3 games."
  • 2It generates an overlay showing that result.
  • 3Drop the overlay into OBS and play out the challenge on stream.
